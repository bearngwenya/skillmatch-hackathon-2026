# SkillMatch - AI-Powered Career Opportunity & Skill Matching

SkillMatch is a human-centred career navigation prototype created for the 10Alytics x JengaGlobal Q3 Students Hackathon 2026, Product Design track.

## The problem

AI and automation are changing how people work, but many workers struggle to understand how their existing experience and transferable capabilities can lead to new career opportunities. Traditional career tools often rely heavily on job titles, generic skills lists, or personality-style quizzes, which can produce recommendations that feel deterministic or disconnected from real work evidence.

## The solution

SkillMatch starts with the work a person actually does. It helps users:

- describe their current role, experience, and changing work context
- identify transferable capabilities from real work evidence
- confirm, edit, or reject AI-generated interpretations
- explore multiple possible career paths rather than one "destiny" result
- understand why each recommendation appeared
- see the skills they already have and the gaps they may need to close
- create a practical next-step development plan
- save career paths for later comparison

## Core product principle

**Recommend. Explain. Let the user challenge it. Then refine.**

SkillMatch should not tell users who they are. It should help them understand what their evidence suggests, why it suggests it, and what they can do next.

**AI recommends. You decide.**

## Prototype scope

The high-fidelity Figma prototype contains 8 main screens and 3 supporting interaction states:

1. Profile Setup
2. Experience Input
3. AI Skill Discovery
4. Career Opportunities
5. Why This Fits
6. Skills & Gaps
7. Next-Step Plan
8. Saved Paths

Supporting states include correction feedback, dismiss confirmation, and recommendation provenance.

## Research and testing

The concept was developed using a human-centred product design process. Early formative testing with two users showed value in discovering career options they may not have considered, while also highlighting information density and recommendation clarity as areas to improve. Those findings informed the final interaction hierarchy and explainability model.

## Responsible AI approach

SkillMatch treats AI outputs as interpretations, not facts. Users can review, edit, confirm, or reject inferred capabilities before they influence downstream recommendations. Recommendations are designed to explain their reasoning and avoid employment guarantees or deterministic career labels.

## Technical implementation concept

This prototype demonstrates the complete interaction logic from user evidence to AI interpretation, user confirmation, explainable recommendations, skill-gap analysis, and next-step planning.

A production version could combine:

- user-confirmed experience and capability evidence
- structured occupational and skills data
- local labour-market requirements
- explainable recommendation logic
- user feedback that continuously refines recommendations

## Accessibility and inclusion

The experience prioritises plain language, readable hierarchy, clear controls, progressive disclosure, transferable experience, and respectful language around career change. Colour is not intended to carry meaning on its own, and AI-generated information is separated from user-provided evidence.

## Sustainable Development Goals

## Expected impact

SkillMatch is designed to support:

- improved career discovery
- clearer visibility of transferable skills
- more informed reskilling decisions
- reduced transition uncertainty
- stronger trust in AI-assisted career guidance
